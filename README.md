# 💫 About Me:
Hi! I'm **Ram Kasuru**, an M.S. Applied AI student at **Stevens Institute of Technology** (graduating December 2026). I work on making language models smaller, cheaper and more trustworthy, and on evaluations that catch a false result before it gets reported.

My thesis fine-tuned three small language models (Qwen2.5-3B, Phi-3.5-mini and Granite-3.3-2B) with QLoRA, and found that an apparent ensemble-voting gain was really a schema-compliance artifact. I'm continuing that work with compute-matched experiments across open models. I also quantized the diffusion transformer of Roblox's CubePart to INT4, cutting it from 8.58 GB to 1.48 GB.

**Recent work**
- 🎛️ [MLabs](https://github.com/Ramkasuru/MLabs): a desktop music studio with AI, built with Claude Code (hum-to-instrument, MusicGen)
- 🛡️ [ARC](https://github.com/kayshaah/ARC): neuro-symbolic scam and deceptive-review detection (team project, I designed the Trust Ceiling rule)
- 📄 [Resume Tailor](https://ramkasuru.github.io/resume-tailor): a live web app that tailors a résumé to a job without inventing experience
- 📚 [ramkasuru.com](https://ramkasuru.com): my portfolio, rebuilt as a 3D bookshelf
- 📝 VIOLA, a multimodal video assistant, presented at ISCMCTR 2024

I'm looking for full-time roles in applied AI, ML and evaluation from January 2027, and I'm always glad to connect with people building in this space.
